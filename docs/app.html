A glyph counts its stat only from nodes purchased inside a Manhattan-distance-5 diamond around its socket. A weaker node inside the diamond can beat a stronger one outside it, which is what makes paragon a search problem rather than a shopping list.

Open question

the result worth checking
  • 4 boards beat 5, and that disagrees with the meta At 325 points the generator prefers four boards, because the fifth board’s attachment cost outweighs what it adds. Community Whirlwind builds run five. Either a real finding or a modelling error, and the way to tell is to score a published five-board build with the same objective.
  • Uptime assumptions are estimates How often a build is Berserking or Fortified changes which bonuses win. Kept in a visible table rather than buried, but they are judgement calls.
  • Node growth is greedy, not proven optimal Takes the best marginal node each step. Strong in practice, no guarantee.
